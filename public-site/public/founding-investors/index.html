What Unlock is

Unlock is a decision-intelligence service for high net worth and self-certified sophisticated investors who remain responsible for their own financial decisions.

Founding investors only

It gives them a live view of all their assets: regulated investments such as stocks, pensions and bonds, as well as unregulated investments such as property, gold and crypto. It helps the investor explore consequential decisions with the sources, assumptions, uncertainty and next action kept in view.

Unlock does not

  • Give advice
  • Manage assets
  • Sell products
  • Execute transactions

Who it is for

Unlock is for High Net Worth and self-certified sophisticated investors whose finances have several moving parts: pensions, investments, property, tax and family. Moving into retirement income is one example. Selling a business or receiving an inheritance are others. What matters is joining it all up.

What investors see in Unlock

Unlock tells you when something changes.

i.

Your position moves

A holding falls, a pension rule changes, cash builds up. Unlock tells you straight away.

ii.

Only what matters

Unlock alerts you to changes that matter to your plan, and shows what each one affects, such as retirement income or tax.

iii.

The decision stays yours

An alert points to the question worth looking at, not an answer. What you do about it, and the record of why, stays with you.

What happens next

A decision made in stages, at your pace.

We take it in stages, so you have what you need before you decide.

  1. 01

    The company call (45 minutes)

    We take you through the company: the problem, the opportunity, the product, the team, progress so far and what is still to be proved. It is a two-way conversation, so you can spend time on what matters most to you. You won't be asked for a commitment on the call.

  2. 02

    A written summary, afterwards

    A short document setting out the investment case, for you to read in your own time.

  3. 03

    A deal structure call (30 minutes), if you'd like one

    We go through the exit strategy, the valuation, the risks and what it could return, then how investing works with SeedLegals. You can put figures you choose into an EIS calculator to see the risk and the return side by side.

Before your call

The call is on Google Meet and works best from a computer or iPad, so Tom can share his screen. There's nothing to prepare.

Important information

  • Unlock operates outside the FCA perimeter by design. What we provide is structured decision support, not regulated financial advice.

  • Investing in an early-stage company is high risk. You could lose all the money you invest. Shares in a private company are hard to sell, and any return may take many years or never come.
  • Tax treatment depends on your individual circumstances and on the company and investment continuing to qualify, and the rules can change. Nothing on this page confirms that any tax relief will be available to you.
  • The investment documents govern the terms of any investment and any founding investor benefits.
  • Consider taking independent financial, tax and legal advice before making any investment decision.
