Fragmentation

Investors pay a fragmentation penalty. Their wealth is spread across platforms, wrappers, advisers and assets held directly, with no single view.

Cost of advice

Professional advice on the big decisions is expensive: inheritance tax planning on a £1M estate averages £8,995, and at-retirement advice on a £500K pension £6,700.

Rules keep changing

From April 2026 business relief is capped at £2.5M per person, and from April 2027 pensions come into inheritance tax. More households need to plan.

The product

What Unlock does.

A live view of all your assets: regulated investments such as stocks, pensions and bonds, and unregulated ones such as property, gold and crypto. Unlock tells you when something changes, and helps you think the decision through. The decision stays yours.

Asset register

Everything you own in one place, with the evidence behind it: pensions, investments, property, cash and the harder-to-value things.

Alerts

A holding rises or falls, a pension rule changes, cash builds up. Unlock tells you straight away, and only about changes that matter to your plan.

Retirement income planner

What income your assets could provide over time, with past market crashes such as 2008 replayed against the plan.

EIS modeller

What investing in EIS companies each year might look like over 15 to 25 years, including loss relief and realistic failure rates.

Due Diligence tool

A company's claims checked against the evidence, the risks and the open questions. An absence of red flags, never a green light.

Heartbeat

If you don't log in for a set period, Unlock contacts someone you have chosen in advance, so the people closest to you can find your financial picture.

Unlock operates outside the FCA perimeter by design. What we provide is structured decision support, not regulated financial advice. It does not manage assets, sell products or execute transactions.

The risks, in the same view

For the company

People may not adopt it or pay at the rate planned; the build may take longer or cost more; a small team depends on key people; more capital may be needed; rules and the market may change.

For you

You could lose all the money you invest. Shares in a private company are hard to sell, and any return may take many years or never come. Tax relief depends on your circumstances and the company continuing to qualify.

Possible routes to a return, none guaranteed: secondary sales, a strategic acquisition, dividends, or a listing in the longer term.

Do not invest money you cannot afford to lose.
